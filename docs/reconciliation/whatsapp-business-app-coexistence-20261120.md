## Meta contract and practical impact

Meta explicitly supports one number in the WhatsApp Business app and Cloud API through Coexistence. Nurit can continue 1:1 chats in the app and the two paths mirror those messages. App-originated messages remain free; Cloud API messages use Meta's current per-message, country/category pricing and template/window rules. As of October 2026 Meta provides 1,000 free service messages per number per month; the quoted document templates may be charged as utility messages, so this free service tier must not be used as their price estimate. The Israel utility rate and any partner fee were not established in this audit and must be checked before the transition. Meta requires Business app 2.24.17+, Solution Partner or Tech Provider status, Embedded Signup with session logging and a functioning webhook. v2/v3 Embedded Signup are deprecated; use a supported current version and validate the displayed Business-app onboarding option. See [Meta's Coexistence guide](https://developers.facebook.com/documentation/business-messaging/whatsapp/embedded-signup/onboarding-business-app-users/) and [Meta pricing](https://developers.facebook.com/documentation/business-messaging/whatsapp/pricing/).

Important app effects: 1:1 chat history can be synchronized for the most recent six months with Nurit's consent; group chats stay in the app and do not sync to API. Disappearing, view-once and live-location messages are disabled for 1:1 chats; broadcast lists become read-only. Linked companion devices are unlinked at onboarding; supported devices may be relinked, while WhatsApp for Windows and WearOS are excluded. These are actual product changes even though the primary Business app stays in use. Meta's history synchronization window is 24 hours after onboarding, and `smb_message_echoes` must be digested for app-sent messages. See the same Meta guide, sections “Feature comparison”, “Linked devices” and “Synchronizing WhatsApp Business app data”.
